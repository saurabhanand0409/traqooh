# India Market Study — Offline Advertising Accountability

> Researched 2026-10-04. Scope: what Indian (and India-active) companies offer a **regional advertiser buying offline media** — specifically real-estate developers and education/coaching institutes in Bihar, Jharkhand and UP — and where nobody serves them.
> Tags: **[S]** = from a source listed at the end and checked in this research; **[G]** = general knowledge, not verified here; **[H]** = our hypothesis, to test in customer interviews.
> Companion: `TRAQADVT_COMPETITIVE_ANALYSIS.md` (global and agency view), `TRAQADVT_UNIQUE_OFFER.md` (what we build from this).

---

## 1. Executive summary

1. **Every single capability already exists somewhere in India.** Inventory marketplaces (GoHoardings, The Media Ant, EatAds), print/radio/cinema booking (releaseMyAd, UFO Moviez), OOH operations software with geo-tagged audit photos (Oi Media, Adarth/Moving Walls), independent photo audits (OOHAudit, Proof of Performance), rate benchmarks (Posterscope's rateOOHmeter), lead-source tracking in CRMs (LeadSquared, Sell.Do), and missed-call tracking numbers (Exotel). Programmatic digital screens (Lemma, AdOnMo, Moving Walls) and audience currency (RoadStar) cover the metro/digital end.
2. **"We take GPS-stamped photos" is not a differentiator.** Oi Media markets "tamper-proof" geo-tagged, time-stamped audit reports; The Media Ant shares execution images and an "exhibition certificate"; OOHAudit has done independent geo-tagged audits since 2012.
3. **The industry's own 2026 diagnosis is a trust deficit with five failures**: inflated traffic claims, late installation and early removal, one billboard sold to several clients, swapped or worse locations, and **fake, recycled or AI-edited proof photos**. Geo-tagging alone does not stop the last four.
4. **Nobody connects the four questions a regional advertiser actually asks about each rupee:** *Did it run as promised? Was the price fair? Was it legal? What did it produce?* Each is answered, partially, by a different company. The full row is answered by none.
5. **Regulators have created new, local, urgent needs in the exact ICP:**
   - Patna Municipal Corporation's March 2026 order: every hoarding must be registered (ward, size, GPS, owner consent, structural certificate) or be treated as illegal. Only 56 advertisers had complied (~11.1 lakh sq ft), with no digital registry. Bihar's government has warned that *advertisers* on unauthorised displays face legal action.
   - Bihar RERA: every real-estate ad must show the RERA registration number and website (agents also a QR code). Promoters have been penalised.
   - CCPA's coaching-ad guidelines (in force since 13 Nov 2024): no guaranteed-result or rank claims, written consent for toppers' photos. 48 coaching centres have been fined.
   No platform found links **what was actually displayed** to these rules.
6. **Measurement is weakest exactly where the ICP spends.** Radio audience measurement (RAM) covers 13 cities, not Patna; smaller towns rely on a print survey that broadcasters say under-counts radio by up to 30%. In regional markets, **the advertiser's own enquiries are the only reliable measure**, and nobody packages that for offline media.

## 3. The buyer's journey and who serves each step

A regional advertiser's offline campaign goes through nine steps:

| # | Step | Who serves it today (India) | What's missing for the ICP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decide budget and mix | Agencies (big clients), The Media Ant planners (SMEs), gut feel | Evidence from their own past results |
| 2 | Find inventory | GoHoardings (1.2 lakh sites, 130 cities), The Media Ant (350k+ options, 12 verticals), EatAds, releaseMyAd (212 newspapers, 27 radio stations, 19 multiplex chains), UFO Moviez (3,821 screens, 1,382 towns) | Licence/compliance status of the hoarding |
| 3 | Judge the price | Listed rates (GoHoardings, Media Ant), rateOOHmeter (Posterscope, agency tool) | Neutral, transaction-based local ranges available to independents |
| 4 | Check the creative is legal | Lawyers, in-house teams | RERA / CCPA checks tied to the actual artwork and the actual display |
| 5 | Book | Marketplaces, agencies, direct with media owners | (adequately served) |
| 6 | Get it installed on time | Media owner's crew; ops software (Oi Media, Adarth, TraqOOH) | Independent evidence of install date, not the vendor's word |
| 7 | Prove it ran as promised | Vendor photos (Media Ant), geo-tagged app photos (Oi Media), independent bureaus (OOHAudit, POP) | Defence against recycled/edited photos, shared boards, substituted sites; display-days delivered; automatic make-good |
| 8 | Measure what it produced | CRMs with source tracking (LeadSquared, Sell.Do), missed-call numbers (Exotel), portals' dashboards | One view of **cost per real enquiry/site visit/admission per medium**, offline included, joined to verified delivery |
| 9 | Decide next season | Agencies' memory, spreadsheets | A learning record across campaigns and benchmarks from similar advertisers |

**Steps 3, 4, 7, 8 and 9 are where the ICP is under-served, and they are connected:** the same campaign record can answer all five if it is set up for it.

## 5. Evidence of pain

**The five trust failures (industry analysis, Aug 2026) [S]:**
1. Inflated traffic data presented without independent verification.
2. Late installation, and creatives removed before the contracted end "often without informing the client".
3. The same structure sold to several clients at once.
4. Poorer placement than promised, or a substituted location.
5. Fake or manipulated proof: recycled photos, stock images, AI-edited images.

Its conclusion: "independent audience data, third-party campaign verification, standardized reporting, and professional auditing practices are no longer optional."

**Price opacity [S]:** buying "largely governed by the whim and fancy of the media asset owner"; rates vary by campaign and buyer; IOAA can only *recommend* indicative pricing.

**Attribution [S]:** real-estate marketers call attribution "a key pain point", and are moving to cost-per-site-visit and cost-per-booking because most portal leads aren't buyers.

**Unmeasured media [S]:** no RAM in Patna; IRS reportedly under-counts small-town radio.

## 6. Regulatory landscape for the ICP (Bihar focus)

| Rule | What it requires | Who is exposed | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Patna Municipal Corporation hoarding order** (12 Mar 2026) | Register every hoarding: site, ward, dimensions, area, property type, owner consent, owner contact, **lat/long**, structural stability certificate; physical + digital submission; unregistered = illegal under the Bihar Municipal Act 2007 | Agencies, media owners, building owners | [S] |
| PMC licence fee | Supreme Court direction: ₹10/sq ft per year licence fee, with interest on existing hoardings; PMC hasn't collected since 2014 (estimated ₹70–120 cr/year lost); draft Advertisement (Amendment) Regulations 2025 | Media owners/agencies | [S] |
| **Bihar illegal-hoarding crackdown** | Action on 200+ illegal hoardings, notices for 250+; minister warns *advertisers* responsible for unauthorised displays face legal action | **Advertisers** | [S] |
| **Bihar RERA advertising rules** | RERA registration number and RERA website on every promoter ad; agents also a QR code; penalties for non-compliance; fake RERA numbers treated as serious fraud (₹90 lakh penalty in one case) | Real-estate developers, agents | [S] |
| MahaRERA (for comparison) | Registration number + QR linking to the project page on all ads incl. hoardings; ₹50,000 penalty | Developers in Maharashtra | [S] |
| **CCPA coaching guidelines** (from 13 Nov 2024) | No guaranteed selection/rank/job claims; written consent before using toppers' photos/testimonials; disclose course, fee and refund terms; covers all ad forms | Coaching institutes | [S] |
| CCPA enforcement | ₹54.6 lakh in fines across 48 coaching centres | Coaching institutes | [S] |

**Nobody found offers:** a hoarding registry/compliance status per site for advertisers, RERA/CCPA checks on the artwork, or evidence that **the displayed** ad (not just the approved file) was compliant.

## 8. What is honestly not unique about us

- Geo-tagged, time-stamped proof photos: **not unique** (Oi Media, Adarth, OOHAudit, Media Ant's vendor photos).
- An OOH inventory and booking platform: **not unique** (GoHoardings, Media Ant, EatAds).
- Print/radio/cinema booking: **not unique** (releaseMyAd, Media Ant, UFO direct).
- Lead-source tracking: **not unique** (LeadSquared, Sell.Do, Exotel).
- A rate benchmark: **not unique in concept** (rateOOHmeter).

What is new is **combining them per rupee, for the regional enquiry-driven advertiser, with protections against the 2026 trust failures and with the local regulations built in.** That is the subject of `TRAQADVT_UNIQUE_OFFER.md`.
