# TraqAdvt — Go-to-Market

> Status: proposal (2026-10-04). Built on the ICP in `TRAQADVT_PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md` and the founder's questionnaire (Phase 1 states: Bihar, UP, Jharkhand, Delhi NCR; target start: early November 2026; no pilot customer named yet).

---

## 1. Beachhead

**Where:** Patna and Bihar first (BrandSculpt's home market and network), then Jharkhand and UP, then Delhi NCR.
**Who:** real-estate developers and education/coaching institutes, reached through regional agencies (BrandSculpt first).
**Why this order:** the founder has relationships and inventory there; competitors' focus is metros and digital screens; enquiry-driven categories make outcomes measurable from the first campaign.

## 2. The first 90 days (pilot)

| Week | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Pick **3 design-partner advertisers** from BrandSculpt's current or recent clients (at least one real-estate project, one coaching institute) | Names, dates, budgets |
| 0–2 | Ship field app v2, trust cleanup, campaign brief + response paths | Product ready for a closed-loop campaign |
| 2–4 | Set up each campaign with objective, KPI, budget, unique tracking numbers / QR per medium, a lead register at the front desk | Every campaign "closed-loop" from day one |
| 4–10 | Run campaigns: verified installation within 48 h, audits mid-flight, weekly outcome entries | Live tracking link + weekly lead counts by medium |
| 10–12 | Deliver a **Proof of Display + Results report** per campaign; ask what it was worth and whether they'd pay | 3 case studies, pricing evidence, testimonials (with permission) |

**Offer to design partners:** free verified proof and results reporting for one campaign, in exchange for outcome data, feedback and (if results allow) a named case study.

## 3. Sales motion

1. **Founder-led selling** to regional agencies and their clients. The demo is a real PoD report and a real cost-per-enquiry-by-medium report, not slides.
2. **Advertiser pull:** the advertiser portal and WhatsApp-delivered reports are seen by every advertiser; each report carries "Verified by TraqAdvt" and a verification link. Advertisers who like it ask their *other* agencies to use it.
3. **Agency channel:** pitch regional agencies as "show clients proof and results; stop arguing about whether sites went up".
4. **Industry presence (later):** regional builder and education associations, and local events, using pilot case studies.

## 4. Messaging

| Audience | Pain | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Advertiser (real estate / education) | "I pay lakhs for hoardings and newspaper ads and don't know what worked" | "See every site's proof, what it cost, and how many enquiries each medium brought." |
| Agency | Disputes, manual photo reports on WhatsApp, price pressure | "Proof your clients trust, reports in one click, and evidence that your plan worked." |
| Media owner | Late payments and disputes over whether a site was displayed | "Verified display ends the argument." (Later: free inventory listing.) |

## 5. Channels and tactics

- WhatsApp-first: reports, proof links and reminders go where advertisers already are.
- Hindi and English throughout (field app, reports, emails).
- A simple public page per case study (with permission).
- No paid marketing until a sales motion converts in person.

## 6. Pricing at launch

Per `TRAQADVT_MONETIZATION.md`: pilot free or discounted; then agency platform fee + per-verified-site fee. Advertiser access is free. Invoice and bank transfer for the first customers; Razorpay later.

## 7. Before any outside customer sees the product

- Remove fabricated claims (chatbot, landing stats, testimonials) and the fake payment page.
- Decide the brand (TraqOOH vs. TraqAdvt vs. a new name) and the legal entity. This affects the domain, emails, the app's package name and the Play Store listing (organisation account needs a D-U-N-S number in that entity's exact name).
- Privacy policy and terms naming the actual entity, covering field-worker location data and advertisers' lead data.
- A one-page pilot agreement (scope, data use, confidentiality, outcome-data consent).

## 8. Metrics

| Metric | Why | Target for end of pilot |
|---|---|---|
| **Closed-loop campaigns** (north star) | The moat | ≥ 3 |
| Verified-on-time rate | Core promise | ≥ 90% within 48 h of start |
| Outcome capture rate | Share of campaigns with response paths and weekly entries | 100% of pilot campaigns |
| Report-to-payment conversion | Willingness to pay | ≥ 1 paid commitment |
| Advertiser report opens | Pull | Every report opened; ≥ 1 forwarded |
| Time to plan (later, Phase 3) | Planner value | Measured before/after |

## 9. What would make us rethink

- No advertiser agrees to set up response paths → outcome capture needs a different design (e.g. CRM-only totals plus experiments).
- Advertisers value proof but not results → stay a verification product longer; delay the planner.
- Agencies resist transparency → sell directly to advertisers instead.
