Platform Features

Everything You Need to
Run OOH Campaigns

From adding a site to finalising a campaign — TraqOOH handles every step of the outdoor advertising workflow.

Inventory Management

Add and manage all your OOH sites — hoardings, billboards, unipoles, LED boards. Track size, lighting, facing, rates, vendor and live availability status.

Campaign Management

Create campaigns, link sites, assign booking dates and track status from DRAFT to LIVE. Full campaign history with site counts and cost tracking.

Advertiser Portal

Generate a secure access link and share it with your client. They can view proposed sites, shortlist favourites, set dates and finalize the campaign — without logging in.

Site Availability Tracking

Real-time availability status — Vacant, Vacant Soon, or Booked. Set availability dates per site. Instantly see what's available for a new campaign.

Media Planning & Cost Sheets

Add sites to campaigns in bulk, set per-site printing and mounting costs, generate professional cost sheets with grand total for client proposals.

Multi-Role Access

Separate logins for Admins, Employees and Advertisers. Employees see only their own campaigns. Admins see everything. Advertisers access only their proposals.
